# Support and Bug Reports

For feedback, bug reports or support, use either of these official channels:

- The comments section on the [official MXPulse page at MXB-Mods](https://mxb-mods.com/mxpulse/)
- Discord: `ksost_`

Please include:

- MXPulse version
- MX Bikes session type: Practice, Qualifying, Warm-up or Race
- Offline or online session
- Approximate number of riders
- Screen resolution and aspect ratio
- A clear description of what happened
- Steps that reproduce the issue
- Screenshot or short video when possible

Do not upload personal configuration files without checking their contents first.
